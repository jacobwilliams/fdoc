    fdoc_highlight.f90

    modulefdoc_highlight

    Syntax highlighting of Fortran, from fformat's tokens. Classes: k keywords and intrinsics, c comments, d doc comments, s strings, n numbers, p preprocessor lines.

    JSON and TOML get a simple scan of their own, with the same classes: k keys, s strings, n numbers, dates and literals such as true, p TOML table headers and c TOML comments.
